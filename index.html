01 / About

Who I Am

I am a second-year (3rd Semester) Computer Science and Engineering student at Lovely Professional University, and I have come to understand something about myself early on: I learn best when there is a real problem in front of me and code on the screen.

My academic journey gave me the foundations — data structures, algorithms, databases, networks. But the real learning happened outside the classroom: building a full-stack community management system from scratch, experimenting with multilingual voice AI, tracking habits in a web app I actually use daily.

I am drawn to the intersection of practical software engineering and real human problems. Whether it is a gated community needing a digital backbone or a restaurant wanting AI that understands regional languages — I am interested in understanding the problem before reaching for a framework.

Right now I am focused on deepening my backend and systems knowledge, exploring AI applications, and contributing to projects that matter beyond the grade sheet.

04 / Projects

What I Have Built

Each project started with a real problem. These are the solutions I built to solve them.

01

Gated Community Management System

FastAPI SQLAlchemy SQLite HTML/CSS/JS JWT PWA
View on GitHub
The Problem

Residential communities struggle with fragmented communication — notices go unread, visitor logs are manual, polls happen over WhatsApp, and property listings have no organized system. Residents and admins waste time on coordination that should be automatic.

My Solution

A full-stack web application that gives gated communities a single digital platform. Admins manage the community; residents interact with it. Every feature was designed around actual community workflows, not abstract CRUD operations.

Key Features

🔐JWT Authentication + Role-Based Access Control
💬Resident-to-Resident Messaging System
📊Community Polls and Voting
🏠Property Listings Module
🔔Real-time Notifications
🌍Multilingual PWA (offline-capable)
What I Learned

Building this taught me what "full-stack" actually means in practice — not just front end and back end, but thinking about authentication flows, database schema design, API contract design, and UX all at once. The multilingual PWA requirement pushed me to understand service workers and internationalization beyond theory.

02

AI Voice Receptionist

Speech-to-Text Text-to-Speech AI Conversation Telephony API Backend APIs Database
View on GitHub
The Problem

Small restaurants and local businesses in India often lose customers during peak hours because phone calls go unanswered. Hiring additional staff only for call handling is expensive. Existing AI solutions do not understand regional languages naturally.

My Solution

An AI-powered voice receptionist that handles incoming calls, understands spoken regional languages, takes orders naturally through conversation, and confirms details — all without a human agent on the line.

Key Features

🎙️Multilingual Speech Recognition (regional languages)
🤖AI-powered Natural Conversation Flow
🔊Natural-sounding Text-to-Speech Responses
📞Telephony Integration for Real Calls
📦Real-time Order Processing and Database Storage
🌐Backend API for Business Dashboard
What I Learned

This project exposed me to challenges I had not anticipated — regional language nuance in speech recognition, managing latency in voice pipelines, and designing conversation flows that feel natural rather than scripted. It also showed me that AI integration is as much about the system design around the AI as the AI itself.

03

Habit Tracker

HTML/CSS JavaScript Local Storage Responsive Design
View on GitHub
The Problem

Most habit apps are over-engineered or require an account before you can try them. I wanted something lightweight, private, and immediately useful — where the focus is the habit, not the app.

My Solution

A clean, browser-based habit tracker that works without a backend or account. Create habits, check them off daily, see streaks build up, and understand patterns through simple visual data.

Key Features

✅Habit Creation and Daily Check-off
🔥Streak and Consistency Tracking
📱Fully Responsive (mobile-first)
🔒Private by Design — data stays in the browser
What I Learned

Small projects teach different lessons. This one taught me about user experience constraints — how much state management is needed even for a simple application, and why consistent data modeling matters even in a localStorage context.

09 / Community

Beyond the Classroom

Learning should extend beyond code. Community work and social problem-solving inform how I think about technology.

🌱

Environmental Awareness Campaign

Indus, Hanuma, & Shastra Schools

Context

Organized an Environmental Awareness Campaign in Suryapet, Telangana, targeting 500–600 school students from Classes 3 to 10 across three different schools (Indus School, Hanuma High School, and Shastra School) as part of a Community Development Project.

Problem Identified

Observed a gap between students' theoretical knowledge of environmental issues and practical action. Students needed to understand how their individual choices and daily habits contribute to larger environmental problems.

Work Performed

Approached school authorities to organize awareness sessions. Conducted interactive presentations to make environmental concepts relatable for different age groups, and mobilized students to participate in the Times Critical Thinking Championship (TCTC) Online Quiz.

Impact

Successfully delivered direct environmental awareness to over 500 students, encouraging them to discuss and connect environmental responsibility to their everyday actions outside of a strict academic environment.

Reflection

Learned that community work requires persistence and flexibility, especially when facing institutional roadblocks. The experience significantly improved my communication skills across different age groups and boosted my confidence in organizing and public speaking.
